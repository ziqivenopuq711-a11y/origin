Bug Bounty Rewards

Understand how rewards are calculated, what severities are eligible, and how payouts are processed.

💰 Reward Overview

Origin Protocol provides rewards in OUSD on Ethereum, denominated in USD. Rewards are distributed according to the impact of the vulnerability, assessed using the Immunefi Vulnerability Severity Classification System V2.2.

This is a simplified 5-level scale with separate tracks for smart contracts/blockchains and websites/applications. The scale covers everything from the consequence of exploitation to the privilege required and the likelihood of a successful exploit being executed in production.

Payouts are handled directly by the Origin Protocol team and are denominated in USD, paid in OUSD. No KYC information is required for payout processing.

⚖ Critical Reward Calculation

Critical smart contract vulnerabilities are subject to a reward cap of 10% of the economic damage caused, with a maximum payout of $1,000,000 USD.

There is a guaranteed minimum of $50,000 USD for any confirmed Critical smart contract vulnerability, regardless of the funds-at-risk calculation. The primary consideration for the reward amount is the funds directly affected, along with PR and brand impact considerations at the discretion of the Origin Protocol team.

For example: if a vulnerability puts $500,000 in user funds at risk, the reward would be $50,000 (10% cap). If a vulnerability risks $20,000,000, the reward would be $1,000,000 (max cap). A vulnerability risking only $200,000 would still receive the $50,000 minimum.

🔄 Payout Process

Once a valid vulnerability is confirmed, payouts follow a standardised process managed by Immunefi on behalf of Origin Protocol. Understanding this process will help you set expectations after submitting a report.

1

Submit Report

Submit your vulnerability report through the Immunefi platform with a complete PoC and impact assessment.

2

Triage & Validation

The Origin Protocol security team reviews and validates the report. Managed triage is enabled — average resolution time is 8 hours.

3

Severity Assessment

The team assesses the severity and calculates the reward using the Immunefi V2.2 classification system.

4

Payout in OUSD

Reward is denominated in USD and paid in OUSD on Ethereum. No KYC is required. Payout is processed through Immunefi.
