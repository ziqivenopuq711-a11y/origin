ℹ Information

Origin Protocol is a suite of complimentary DeFi products designed to increase economic opportunity for all. These permissionless and composable smart contracts provide superior user experiences across DeFi in a groundbreaking multichain yield ecosystem.

Origin Dollar (OUSD) is a fully backed stablecoin initially launched in September 2020 on the Ethereum network. Its design is superior to existing stablecoins because OUSD captures competitive yields while being passively held in wallets. For more information visit ousd.com.

This bug bounty program is focused on their smart contracts and application, with the primary goal of preventing: loss of funds, freezing of funds that cannot be undone by admin actions, unauthorized access to admin functions, and governance process failures.

Rewards are denominated in USD and paid out in OUSD on Ethereum, according to the impact of the vulnerability and the Immunefi Vulnerability Severity Classification System V2.2.

🎯 Scope

The program covers smart contracts deployed on Ethereum mainnet and web/application assets. Both smart contract vulnerabilities and website/application vulnerabilities are in scope, subject to the impact categories defined below.

In-Scope Technologies

Solidity Ethereum OUSD OETH OGN Staking Vault Strategies Flipper Web App API

Known Out-of-Scope Issues

The following issues are considered out of scope and will not be rewarded:

  • All issues found in past audits at docs.ousd.com/security-and-risks/audits
  • Attacks that only change the mix of backing stablecoins without reducing total value
  • Reductions in backing stablecoins due to normal governance or strategy fees
  • Intentional rounding behaviour in the Flipper contract
  • Anything airdrop-related in the old OGN staking contract at 0x501804B374EF06fa9C427476147ac09F1551B9A0

📋 Program Rules

Primacy of Impact

Origin Protocol adheres to the Primacy of Impact for Smart Contract Critical severity. If an impacted asset is not explicitly listed in scope but is owned by the project, it is still considered in-scope as long as the impact falls within the Critical severity level. All other levels are governed by the Primacy of Rules.

KYC & Proof of Concept

No KYC information is required for payout processing. However, a Proof of Concept (PoC) is always required for all severity levels before a report will be considered for a reward.

Prohibited Activities

  • Any testing on mainnet or public testnet — all testing must be done on local forks
  • Testing with pricing oracles or third-party smart contracts
  • Phishing or social engineering attacks against Origin Protocol employees or customers
  • Testing with third-party systems, browser extensions, SSO providers, or ad networks
  • Denial-of-service attacks against project assets
  • Automated testing that generates significant traffic volumes
  • Public disclosure of an unpatched vulnerability in an embargoed bounty

Critical Reward Cap

Critical vulnerabilities are capped at 10% of economic damage, with the main consideration being the funds affected along with PR and brand impact, at the discretion of the Origin Protocol team. There is a minimum payout of USD 50,000 for Critical smart contract bug reports. Payouts are handled directly by Origin Protocol and denominated in USD, paid in OUSD.
